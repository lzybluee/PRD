Rings, Rods, and Staves

Discover and array of specific magic rings, rods, and staves, organized by type and presented alongside handy tables organizing them by monetary value, allowing you to find gear in your price range at a glance. Whether you're a monk looking for a magic ring to protect you as you charge unarmored into battle, or a spellcaster eager to dominate the battlefield with a mighty staff of power, this section is for you.
